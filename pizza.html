About Pizza

Pizza is a famous Italian dish made with dough, cheese, and tomato sauce. It is one of the most popular foods in the world. Are you craving delicious pizza but don't want to order from outside? This easy Homemade Pizza Recipe is perfect for beginners and can be made with simple ingredients available at home.It is fresh,healthy and customizeable according to your taste.

Ingredients

For Pizza Dough:

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour(maida)
  • 1 teaspoon sugar
  • 1 taspoon salt
  • 1 teaspoon yeast
  • 2 teaspoon oil
  • watm water(as required)

For Pizza Sauce:

  • 2 tomatoes(pureed)
  • 1 tablespoon oil
  • 2 garlic cloves(chopped)
  • 1 teaspoon oregano
  • 1 teaspoon chili flakes
  • salt to taste

For Toppings

  • Capsicum(bell papers)
  • Onion
  • Sweet corn
  • Olives
  • Mozzarella cheeze

    • Steps

    Step1: Prepare the dough

    1. Take warm water in a bowl and mix sugar and yeast.
    2. Let it rest for 5-10 minutes until it becomes foamy.
    3. Now mix flour,salt and oil in another bowl.
    4. Add yeast mixture and knead into a soft dough.
    5. Cover and let it rise for 1-2 hours.

    Step2:Make Pizza Sauce

    1. Heat oil in a pan and sautè garlic.
    2. Add tomato puree and cook for5-7minutes
    3. Add salt,oregano and chili flakes.
    4. Cook until thick and let it cool

      5. Enjoy Your Day
