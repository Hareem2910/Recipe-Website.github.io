About Burger

Burger is one of the most popular fast foods in the world. It is made with a juicy patty, fresh vegetables, and soft buns. Perfect for a quick and tasty meal.

Ingredients

  • Burger buns
  • Chicken or beef patty
  • Lettuce
  • Tomato slices
  • Cheese
  • Ketchup & Mayonnaise

Steps

  1. Cook the burger patty
  2. Toast the buns lightly
  3. Add lettuce, tomato, and cheese
  4. Place the patty inside the bun
  5. Add sauces and close the burger
  6. Serve hot and enjoy
