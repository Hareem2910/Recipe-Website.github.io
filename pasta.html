About Pasta

Pasta is a classic Italian dish loved all over the world. It can be made with creamy white sauce or tangy tomato sauce. It is simple, delicious, and perfect for any time.

Ingredients

  • Pasta
  • Milk or cream
  • Cheese
  • Garlic
  • Butter
  • Vegetables or chicken

Steps

  1. Boil pasta in salted water
  2. Prepare sauce (white or red)
  3. Add vegetables or chicken
  4. Mix pasta with sauce
  5. Add cheese on top
  6. Serve hot and enjoy
